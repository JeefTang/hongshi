Application · desalination

Seawater desalination booster pumps

RO feed and interstage booster duty at 5–7 MPa. Here the design driver is not efficiency alone — it is corrosion.

The duty

A seawater RO train needs a high-pressure feed pump at 5.0–7.0 MPa, held steady as membrane resistance rises with fouling and temperature moves through the year. Interstage boosters add 2–4 MPa between arrays.

Seawater is the hard part. Chlorides attack anything that is not duplex, super duplex or a suitable high alloy — a pump that is efficient but corrodes is a maintenance contract, not an asset.

How we specify it

  • Duplex / super duplex wetted parts (UNS S31803 / S32750) for seawater service.
  • Ceramic or coated plungers with flushed packing or mechanical seals.
  • Flow held constant as membrane resistance rises, trimmed by VFD.
  • Sized for net feed flow, so the energy recovery device works with the pump rather than against it.

Selection range

UDZ 160/16 and UDZ 320/16 at reduced stroke cover 50–500 m³/h per train at 5–7 MPa. Brackish-water duties sit lower, at 2–4 MPa, and use the same family.
