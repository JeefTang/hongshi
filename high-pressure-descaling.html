Application · steel & cleaning

High-pressure descaling and cleaning pumps

20–30 MPa at the descaling header, or 10–40 MPa at the cleaning nozzle. Both punish anything that cannot hold pressure without leaking.

The duty

In hot rolling, the descaling header needs a pressure that stays flat through the pass and recovers at once when the valve opens. Industrial cleaning and water jetting need the same, with a flow that follows the number of nozzles in use.

A centrifugal unit sheds pressure the moment flow jumps at valve opening. On a descaling header that shows up as incomplete scale removal and a patchy strip surface — a quality problem before it is an energy problem.

How we specify it

  • Discharge pressure held within a few percent from no-flow to full nozzle flow.
  • Fast-acting unloader and accumulator sized with the header, not bolted on.
  • Hardened plungers and valve seats for water carrying mill scale.
  • Skid-mounted with filtration, relief valve and local controls.

Selection range

UDZ 100/16 to UDZ 320/16 with high-pressure trim cover 20–200 m³/h at 10–40 MPa; special trims to 70 MPa on request. The skid is built around the duty cycle, because a descaling pump spends its life starting and stopping.
