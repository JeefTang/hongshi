Boiler feed pumps for industrial and waste-heat boilers

Steady flow, high pressure, and 8,000 hours a year. Feed duty is where a few points of efficiency turn into money.

The duty

A feed pump works against drum pressure plus feedwater line losses — typically 4–16 MPa for industrial boilers and waste-heat units — while the steam load moves above it. The pump itself runs almost continuously.

That is why efficiency, not first cost, sets the operating bill: a unit that loses five points of efficiency costs more in a year than a large part of its purchase price.

Why positive displacement

  • Volumetric efficiency stays above 90% as clearances open with age — the machine still delivers its rated flow years later.
  • Flow is set by speed, not by the system curve, so drum level control stays stable when load drops.
  • Low NPSH requirement, which matters with hot deaerated water.
  • VFD speed control for wide turn-down without recirculation losses.

Reference: 120 t/h and 130/160 t/h boiler feed replacements in industrial plants, measured at 21% and 19% energy saving. A coke dry quenching waste-heat boiler feed replacement measured 26.7% saving with a 3.2-year payback.

Selection range

UDZ 100/16 and UDZ 160/16 cover most industrial boiler duties: 100–400 m³/h at 4–16 MPa. Where drum pressure or feed line length pushes beyond that, we extend the same family rather than change architecture.
