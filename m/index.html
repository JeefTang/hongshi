Efficiency you can measure, maintenance you can plan

Why operators switch

A multistage centrifugal pump in abrasive or high-pressure service typically runs at 45–65% efficiency once wear rings open up. A positive-displacement plunger pump holds 90%+ because its volumetric efficiency does not depend on fine clearances.

  • Direct mechanical drive. No hydraulic power pack, no high-speed friction pairs, no oil overheating in summer.
  • Drop-in retrofit. Matches the piping orientation of the centrifugal pump, so the change is a swap — not a rebuild.
  • Predictable wear. Plungers, valve seats and seals are consumables with published intervals; the crank, crosshead and frame are not.
  • Smart control. VFD, condition monitoring, fault diagnosis and remote data as an option on every unit.
