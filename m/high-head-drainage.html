Application · water transfer

High-head drainage and mountain irrigation pumps

Lifting water several hundred metres — mountain irrigation, plateau supply, high-lift drainage. The static head does the work; the pump has to do it efficiently.

The duty

Mountain irrigation and plateau water supply need lifts of 300–2,500 m, often over long pipelines, with reservoir levels that move through the season. Past a certain lift a single centrifugal stage cannot reach, and multistage units stack losses stage after stage.

Because static head dominates, every point of pump efficiency is multiplied across the whole lift — and the pipeline has to be protected against surge when the pump stops.

How we specify it

  • Series staging: two or more plunger pumps in series where the lift exceeds one unit's pressure rating.
  • Check valves and surge protection sized together with the pipeline, not added afterwards.
  • Efficiency held at part load, since irrigation demand varies through the season.
  • Hardened plungers and valve seats for water carrying sand or silt.

Selection range

UDQ 60 through UDZ 600, single unit or series staged: 50–500 m³/h at 300–2,500 m of head, discharge up to 25 MPa. The number of stages is set by the lift, not by a catalogue page.
