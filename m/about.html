About us

More than twenty years of industrial engineering, now focused on fluid machinery

DALIAN HONGSHI TECHNOLOGY was founded in Dalian in 1999. Since 2020 our Fluid Machinery Division has built packaged plunger pumps and slurry pumps for industrial projects and for export.

Background

The company was founded on 26 November 1999 in Dalian, Liaoning Province, and began in industrial automation products and system integration. Over more than two decades we have accumulated practical engineering experience across industrial plants, serving machinery manufacturing, shipbuilding, automotive, electronics, mining, petrochemical and tobacco industries.

In 2020, as the business grew, we established the Fluid Machinery Division, which handles the packaged manufacturing and international trade of fluid machinery equipment. Our fluid machinery factories are located in Lüshun (Dalian) and Xuancheng (Anhui).

The division works on high-speed direct drive and low-speed high-torque drive machines — the two architectures behind every pump we supply.

What we have delivered

  • Boiler feed — 120 t/h and 130/160 t/h units replacing multistage centrifugal feed pumps in industrial boilers, 21% and 19% energy saving.
  • Mine dewatering — three pump houses at −50, −250 and −500 m, six machines replacing 24 centrifugal units, 22–37% saving.
  • Coke dry quenching waste-heat boiler — feed pump replacement, 26.7% saving, 3.2-year payback.
  • Oilfield injection — seven-plunger station at Shengli Oilfield, specific power 0.3 kWh/m³·MPa.

How we work

Most of our projects start with a measured duty point rather than a specification. We ask for flow, pressure, hours and a power reading, then propose a machine and a number. If the saving is not there, we say so — a pump that cannot pay for itself is not a project.
