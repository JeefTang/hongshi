Oilfield water injection pumps at 0.3 kWh/m³·MPa

Multistage centrifugal injection pumps typically run at 0.5–0.8 kWh/m³·MPa. Our seven-plunger machines run at 0.3 — and do not need a separate lubrication skid.

The number that decides the project

Injection stations run continuously for years. A difference of 0.2–0.5 kWh/m³·MPa is not a rounding error; on a 300 m³/h station at 20 MPa it is roughly 2,900–7,300 MWh a year.

Multistage centrifugal pumps also have to go back to the factory for overhaul, which means either a spare train or an outage. Plunger pumps are maintained in place.

Configuration

  • UDZ 600 seven-plunger, up to 600 m³/h at 300–630 m head — replaces a 500 m³/h multistage centrifugal pump with one unit
  • VFD or fixed speed, depending on reservoir pressure strategy
  • Smart control: automatic regulation, condition monitoring, fault diagnosis, remote telemetry
  • Skid packaging with suction stabiliser, relief valve and instrumentation

Field reference: Shengli Oilfield, Wanggang injection station — seven-plunger unit commissioned August 2023, in continuous service since. Specific power 0.3 kWh/m³·MPa.
