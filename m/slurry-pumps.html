V5 series · vertical five-plunger

Slurry pumps that hold 83% efficiency on abrasive duty

100–400 m³/h, up to 150 m head, solids above 30% by weight. A mechanical five-plunger pump with ceramic plungers and alloy valve seats — built to replace centrifugal slurry pumps that wear out in weeks and hydraulic ceramic plunger pumps that waste a third of their energy.

The problem

Slurry is where pump economics go wrong

In mineral processing, slurry transport is the acknowledged weak point: centrifugal slurry pumps erode, and the energy bill follows the efficiency curve down.

Wear

15 days to 6 months

Impeller and casing life on abrasive slurry, depending on solids size and velocity. Every rebuild is a production interruption.

Efficiency

25–50%

Actual operating efficiency of a centrifugal slurry pump once wear has opened the clearances. The rest becomes heat and noise.

Energy

$40–50k a year

Annual electricity for a single medium-sized slurry pump at industrial tariffs — most of it lost to inefficiency.

The V5 answer

Positive displacement, engineered for abrasion

Instead of accelerating abrasive solids against a spinning impeller, the V5 displaces them slowly with five vertical plungers. Velocity stays low, so erosion is a function of the materials in contact — not of the whole hydraulic path.

  • Ceramic plungers resist abrasion far beyond coated steel.
  • Alloy valve seats keep volumetric efficiency high as the slurry passes.
  • Mechanical drive — no hydraulic station, no high-speed friction pairs.
  • Vertical layout keeps the footprint small and the suction flooded.

Result: efficiency above 83% — more than 33 percentage points above a centrifugal slurry pump — and roughly half the electricity for the same duty.

Frequently asked

What solids size can it handle?

The V5 is designed for the particle sizes typical of mill discharge, filter press feed and tailings. Send the size distribution with your enquiry — valve and seat selection depends on it.

Is 1.5 MPa enough for our line?

For 100–400 m³/h slurry duties up to 150 m of head, yes. If you need more pressure, the TL multi-plunger range goes to 70 MPa.

How long does a rebuild take?

Wear-part replacement is done in place and is measured in hours, not days. Overhauls at 30,000 hours are planned, not emergency work.
